Fundraising follow-up of the committed interventions (CIV).

- The relationship managers record the pledges of the donors as
  opportunities of the "Intervention fundraising" sales team, linked to an
  intervention. A pledge is not an accounting entry: it is "pledged" once its
  opportunity is won, and "received" as the donations of the same partner
  arrive on the analytic account of the intervention.
- Per intervention, in the company currency: cost (the prefinanced amount on
  the cost accounts, otherwise the commitment in USD at the latest rate),
  received, pledged not yet received, funding gap and coverage. Missing data
  stays "unknown", never 0.
- A "Fundraising" tab on the intervention (priority, deadline, donor types,
  earmarking, ask, impact, stories) and a fundraising closure restricted to the
  fundraising managers.
- A list and pivot of the funding gap, and a "Intervention fundraising"
  spreadsheet dashboard.
