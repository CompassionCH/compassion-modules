In Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > Intervention fundraising, set the
prefixes of the cost accounts (where the interventions are prefinanced) and of
the income accounts (where the donations for the interventions are booked).
For Compassion Switzerland: 5 and 6.

Give the group "Fundraising / User" to the relationship managers and
"Fundraising / Manager" to the people allowed to close the fundraising of an
intervention.
